Which step-navigation pattern?

A

Top stepper (shown above)

All 7 steps visible as a progress bar; click any to jump, done-steps go green. Audience always sees where you are in the story. My recommendation.

B

Slide-deck prev/next

One big scenario at a time with ◀ / ▶ buttons; minimal chrome, most "presentation-like." You lose the at-a-glance map of all 7.

C

Left scenario list

The 7 steps as a vertical list in a second sidebar. More room for long titles, but eats horizontal space the trace wants.
